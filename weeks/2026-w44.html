Special Focus: Brazil Post-Election — Market Winds

  • Day-one rout (26 Oct): Ibovespa fell 13.88% (circuit breaker; worst day since Mar 2020) and the dollar jumped 8.21% to R$5.40, unwinding Flávio-priced long Brazil trades after Lula’s runoff win. link
  • Week close (30 Oct): Ibovespa lost 18.12% on the week—worst since the pandemic—while the dollar rose about 9% to ~R$5.44, more than reversing the first-round rally. link
  • Fiscal signal: Markets demand a credible debt path; PT allies publicly resisted spending cuts (30 Oct), arguing adjustment should hit tax breaks and congressional earmarks instead. link
  • Rates & credit: Continuity lifts long-end DI premiums and hardens Selic-cut expectations, keeping household and SME credit expensive into 2027 while FX pass-through risks keep inflation sticky. link
  • Consumption & delivery: Weaker real and higher funding costs typically squeeze mid-ticket food delivery and Q-commerce; welfare continuity may cushion mass-market orders, but platforms face a tougher promo and inland-expansion P&L. link
