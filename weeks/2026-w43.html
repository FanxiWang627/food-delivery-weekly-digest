Special Focus: Brazil Election — Economic & Business Impact

  • Result (25 Oct): Lula won the runoff with 51.79% (64.3m votes) versus Flávio Bolsonaro’s 48.21%, securing a fourth term; inauguration is set for 5 Jan 2027. link
  • Markets: Investors had priced Flávio after the first round; a Citi manager survey expected >5% Ibovespa losses and >5% real depreciation on a Lula win, with DI Jan-29 rates up >100 bps for most respondents. link
  • Fiscal / rates: Continuity raises fears of persistent deficits and high debt, keeping long-end rates elevated and credit expensive for households and SMEs into 2027. link
  • Consumption & delivery: Higher FX and rates typically squeeze discretionary food delivery and mid-ticket Q-commerce; social-transfer continuity may cushion mass-market order volume while platforms still fund inland expansion and exclusivity litigation. link
  • Competitive overlay: Same-week Keeta–99Food court fights and the ANR suit show merchant multi-homing and onboarding rules remain the near-term battleground regardless of the Planalto outcome. link
